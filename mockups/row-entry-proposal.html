Symptom row — entry state (desktop)

Click any row. Toggle Before / After to compare. In After, click a number to rate; click it again to clear.

What’s busy today

  1. The row doubles in height when opened, so everything below it jumps as you move down the list.
  2. Two things compete on one row: the 14-day chart stays visible above a second line of seven large keys.
  3. The “PM” tag repeats what the ringed pill on the right already says.
  4. A separate Clear key appears and disappears, shifting the keys beside it.
  5. The shortcut hint is repeated inside every open row, right-aligned against the keys — the noisiest part.

Proposal

  • Keys replace the chart, same row. While a row is open its 14-day strip swaps for one flat 0–5 · N/A control (same style as the new toggles). Row height never changes — nothing jumps.
  • The chart isn’t lost: it’s back the moment you move on, and rating auto-advances, so you see each strip again right after you answer.
  • Last time’s value keeps its highlight, as a tinted cell with a colored underline.
  • No PM tag, no Clear key. The ringed pill shows the period; click the selected number again (or press ⌫) to clear — same as Rapid entry.
  • Shortcuts move to one quiet line under the list — where Protocol already keeps its hints — instead of inside the row. My recommendation over removing them: they cost nothing down there, and the arrow/AM-PM keys aren’t guessable. If you’d rather drop them entirely, a ? popover is the fallback.
  • Mobile is unchanged — big keys under the row still make sense for thumbs.
