Where it lives

  • A fourth tab, Sleep, in the desktop toolbar. Mobile stays unchanged.
  • Shown only when Garmin is connected and at least one night has synced. Otherwise the tab is hidden.
  • Clicking the Garmin pill opens this tab.

What changed from Garmi

  • Glimpse's two-layer toolbar replaces Garmi's header. Its night stepper works like the Symptoms date stepper.
  • Blue/Tailwind styling becomes Glimpse's neutrals and violet accent.
  • The 8 metric tiles and the metric tabs merge into one row. Clicking a tile switches the chart.
  • "WHOOP / Garmin" becomes "vs my baseline / vs targets".

New because it's Glimpse

  • Sleep and your symptoms: next-day links between sleep, symptoms and supplements.
  • Your Health score overlaid on any sleep chart.
  • Garmi's login, upload and saved-file panels are dropped. The sync already handles them.
