Audit — what’s wrong today

  1. Date sits where the brand/nav belongs. It’s context for two of three tabs, yet owns the top-left; on Insights it goes invisible and leaves a 280px hole.
  2. Score badge is glued to the tabs. It reads as a fourth tab and pushes the tabs off-center.
  3. Low-frequency utilities get labeled-button weight (Copy 7d, Settings) while there’s no global search.
  4. Two layers, two grids. Bar spans 1400px; the second row is a 640px column — left edges are 250px apart, so nothing lines up.
  5. Second row changes shape per tab. Symptoms: 2 buttons. Protocol: 4 equal-weight buttons + ⋯, with destructive “Clear day” beside “Check all”. “Today’s Notes” says Today while viewing Yesterday.

Redesign — the rules

  • Layer 1 = where am I. Brand left, segmented pill nav dead-center (true 1fr/auto/1fr grid), utilities right as icons. Never changes between tabs.
  • Layer 2 = what am I looking at → what can I do. Left slot is scope (date stepper + progress, or Comparison/Sleep on Insights). Right slot is actions, max one primary.
  • One grid. Both bars and the content share the same 1040px container.
  • ⌘K replaces per-tab search and absorbs Copy / Settings / jump-to-date as commands.
  • Protocol bulk actions collapse into one split button; “Clear day” lives in the menu, in red, behind a divider.
  • Patterns borrowed (Mobbin): OpenAI Platform segmented switcher + right-aligned primary, Descript pill tabs, LangSmith scope-left / range-right filter row, Klaviyo ⌘K bar.
